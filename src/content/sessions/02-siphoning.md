## Introduction

In lectures you were told to bring in old books to siphon as an example.

Just as a reminder, siphoning is a way of enhancing and updating the knowledge
of your CCS in a non-invasive way. One downside is that siphoning takes quite a
lot of time as it is recommended that you look at what you want to siphon (e.g.
an artwork, a page of a textbook, a movie) for a second or more (depending on
how dense the information is) before moving onto the next thing you want to
siphon. This might look like turning a page, looking at the page for a second,
then turning to the next page and so on or looking at a frame of a movie for 30
seconds and then advancing to the next frame. Of course, in terms of movies and
frame-by-frame content, you can easily just retime them to run at say 12 frames
per second (or slower). However, for books automating this process is a bit more
difficult as we'd preferably want to just have them as a pdf document that you
could scroll through, but how to do this conversion with physical books (like
the ones you should have bought in)? This is what you'll be doing today as well
as practicing siphoning — a tedious but necessary process.

## Task 1: Converting physical books to digital documents

Physical books are very easy to find compared to digital versions and some books
may not even have digital incarnations. Hence, to convert your physical books
you will:

1. Use the provided paper guillotines (as a tutor about this), we'll slice off
   the spine of the book. Be careful and you may need to apply quite a bit of
   pressure if the book is thick or the pages are thick.
2. Once you have a stack of pages, then you can put all of them in the paper
   feed tray of the scanner portion of the various printers that are around in
   the buildings (again as a tutor if you can't find any/don't know where they
   are).
3. That's it, the scanning process will work quickly and well, and you'll have a
   pdf in no time. Feel free to dispose of the pages once you're finished.

## Task 2: Your first siphoning

Siphoning is a very simple process. Although again, as we've talked about before
it works best where there's less noise on the wire. So, try not to think of
anything whilst you go through each page within the pdf spending about a second
on each page. If you have an auto-scroller or similar software, you can automate
this process so all you have to do is just sit there with your eyes open and let
the CCS do the work.

Siphoning works in a way similar to how you make memories and remember things,
except instead of these memories being available to you they are made highly
available to the CCS. Again, it's an easy non-invasive way of updating,
reskilling, and enhancing the knowledge that your CCS already comes with.

Remember to ask a tutor if you're having trouble, it can be difficult to
concentrate for that long, but it's a necessary process.

## Bonus Task: Siphon at home

Feel free to siphon other media at home, it'll help with your assignments as
you'll have a better understanding of the techniques and so on. Remember,
siphoning isn't a passive thing like just watching a movie or perhaps reading a
book, it's a very active thing — you must ensure that your thoughts are clear
when you are siphoning in order to get the most out of it. It's difficult, but
practice makes perfect!
